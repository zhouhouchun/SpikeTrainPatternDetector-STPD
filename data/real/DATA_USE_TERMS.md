# Data-use notice for human-derived workbooks

The files under `data/real/` are de-identified human-derived recordings and
frozen annotations made available to reproduce the analyses reported with
STPD. They are not covered by the repository's MIT software licence.

Public access to these files permits inspection and reproduction of the
reported scientific analyses. It does not, by itself, grant a separate licence
for unrelated redistribution, commercial reuse, re-identification, or uses
that conflict with the original consent, institutional requirements, or local
law. Prospective users remain responsible for determining whether their
intended reuse requires permission from the source institution or data
custodian.

Source institution: N. N. Burdenko National Medical Research Center of
Neurosurgery, Moscow, Russia. Alexey Sedov led source-data curation and public
dissemination. Zhou Houchun prepared and audited the repository copy.
