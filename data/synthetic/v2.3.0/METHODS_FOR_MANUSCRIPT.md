# Methods text for manuscript: v2.3 clean-overlap supplement

We generated v2.3.0 as a clean-overlap supplement to the separately frozen v2.2.0 mechanism benchmark. Generation was performed in dimensionless time, `u=t/B`, using shifted-gamma renewal processes. Tonic and Broad HFS shared the same renewal family and refractory term, while pre-frozen `core`, `boundary`, and `deep` mean-ISI strata created controlled overlap between their marginal ISI distributions. Each template contained all three Broad HFS overlap strata, rotated across regular, intermediate, and irregular HFS regimes. The overlap ranges were hidden generator parameters and were not supplied to the detector.

Burst was generated as a finite rate-multiplier pulse on the local renewal background. Each template contained three standalone Bursts and two Bursts nested within Broad HFS. Realized Burst size was frozen at 4–9 spikes. Weak, moderate, and strong pulse strata targeted mean-ISI contrasts of 2.2–3.0, 3.0–4.0, and 4.0–5.5, respectively. Direct standalone and HFS-internal Bursts retained at least three observed context ISIs on each side; in the indivisible two-Burst contextual macro, the separator formed the inner boundary and the outer context shoulder was retained. A latent pulse window was clipped at a retained contextual-run boundary rather than allowed to extend into the explicit separator, and this operation was marked by `Latent_Window_Truncated`. Latent pulse windows, realized spike support, target contrast, and observed shoulder counts were exported separately.

Canonical Pause was not weakened to create overlap. It remained a primary one-ISI gap in `[2.8, 5.0]B`, bounded by two observed spikes, and hard-cut a continuous HFS State. Complex multi-gap Pause remained primary truth. Contextual inter-Burst separators (`0.45–0.65B`) and one-ISI borderline gaps (`0.72–1.55B`) were explicitly generated secondary targets. Both could map to Pause at the product layer, but neither entered primary Pause truth or its macro-average.

To avoid scoring an unobservable boundary, the scheduler inserted an explicit Background run whenever two separately scored macro units with the same primary State label would otherwise touch. This rule did not prohibit direct boundaries between different targets.

Twenty mother templates were generated once and projected exactly by factors of 1, 4, and 10, producing 60 paired detector inputs. Templates 1–5 formed the development set and templates 6–20 the protected holdout set; the scale copies of one `Template_ID` were treated as repeated numerical projections, not independent recordings. Component-keyed random streams isolated Background, Burst, Pause, secondary gaps, Tonic, HFS, and nested-Burst draws.

We retained parallel strict-mechanism and observable-phenotype estimands. Strict truth contained every injected primary Event and generated State. Observable Burst evidence was classified with timestamp-only shifted-gamma null thresholds fitted on development templates and matched by Burst context, realized window size, and available one- or two-sided flank pattern. Flanks were restricted to non-event intervals in the same generator run, preventing a contextual separator or a neighboring Event from supplying evidence. Only clear injected-pulse candidates entered the primary observable Burst estimand; natural HFS null excursions were reported separately. Tonic evidence combined CV/CV2/LV regularity, State length, local flank separation, and overlap-stratum-relative rate. Ambiguous regions were retained and exported in sensitivity masks that supported exclusion and ambiguous-as-positive analyses. These labels are operational outcomes of the pre-specified audit rather than universal claims of biological observability.

A single-ISI threshold baseline was fitted only on development templates and evaluated on holdout templates as a descriptive separability audit. It was not used to accept or reject the generator. No STPD output entered generation, null fitting, phenotype qualification, selection, rejection, or acceptance. No stimulation, slow drift, missed or false spikes, timestamp jitter, sorting error, or other observation noise was simulated. Accordingly, v2.3.0 is a clean engineering supplement, not a realistic acquisition benchmark or a biological gold standard.

The v2.3 HFS support contract was versioned to accommodate the deliberately slower overlap stratum. Cross-version comparisons of the outer HFS State remain meaningful, whereas direct-support F1 is compared only after applying a common support mask. The v2.3 semantic RNG keys also differ from v2.2, so the two releases are separate benchmarks rather than component-for-component counterfactual draws.
