# v2.2 audit response

v2.1 remains frozen. v2.2 makes only the agreed targeted changes:

- Tonic low recall is treated as a generator-phenotype/detector-entry mismatch, not assigned to either side by F1 alone.
- Tonic now exports strict and observable estimands, a continuous evidence score, three evidence classes, reasons and subtype-specific features.
- Existing HFS direct support and State envelope are retained. Added fields describe short interruption role, boundary observability, hard cuts and higher-order Composite Regime relations.
- Canonical Pause splits HFS States and is never direct HFS support.
- Random numbers are component-keyed. A Tonic-only counterfactual test requires all other component-local ISI hashes to remain unchanged.
- The scale design, quota balance, 4-spike boundary Burst, complex Pause, contextual-secondary truth and mechanism/phenotype Burst audit remain intact.

The generator is accepted by structural, reproducibility and semantic contracts, not by improvement in STPD performance.
