# Methods text for manuscript

We generated a clean synthetic mechanism benchmark in dimensionless time using shifted-gamma renewal processes with a shared refractory term. Tonic and Broad HFS were renewal States, Burst was a finite rate-multiplier pulse, and Pause was an explicitly generated observed gap. Burst and HFS could overlap on separate Event and State truth tracks. Contextual inter-Burst separators were retained as secondary targets and excluded from primary Pause truth.

Twenty mother templates were generated and projected exactly by factors of 1, 4 and 10; thus scale copies were paired numerical tests, not independent recordings. Templates 1–5 formed the development set and templates 6–20 the frozen holdout set. Component-keyed random streams isolated Background, Burst, Pause, Tonic, HFS and nested-Burst draws. No detector prediction entered generation, phenotype qualification or rejection.

For Tonic we reported strict mechanism recovery over all generated States and observable-phenotype recovery over states that passed a pre-frozen timestamp-only evidence rule. The continuous evidence score combined CV/CV2/LV regularity, state length, local flank separation and subtype-relative rate band; eligible, ambiguous and no-evidence counts were all retained. Broad HFS truth separately encoded direct support, tolerated short interruptions, nested Burst Events and the outer State envelope. Canonical Pause hard-cut the continuous HFS State, although two flanking States could share a higher-order Composite HFS Regime identifier.

This dataset is an engineering-scale clean synthetic mechanism benchmark and is not a biological gold standard for four neuronal firing modes.
