# Gate B v3.2 scientific semantic overlay

## Status

- Scientific status: active project decision.
- Structural status: additive overlay over the frozen v3.1 table and identity
  contract.
- Authority: this document supersedes only the cross-track scientific clauses
  identified below. It does not rewrite, approve, or re-sign the historical
  v3.1 candidate contract or any v3.1 release artifact.
- Implementation identifier: `stpd_multitrack_v3_2_semantic_overlay`.

The v3.1 normative documents remain byte-preserved historical contracts. Where
their scientific text says that Burst splits Tonic/HFT, or that every Pause must
erase all higher episode context, that text is not the active STPD scientific
policy. The active policy is defined here and in decisions D-021, D-022, D-024,
D-025, and D-030.

## Orthogonal scientific objects

1. Burst is an Event.
2. Tonic and Broad HFS are carrier States.
3. HFT and HF-irregular are frequency-by-regularity subtypes of Broad HFS,
   computed on the same frozen State support.
4. Pause is a Gap.
5. Recurrent Bursting and Recurrent Pausing are descriptive Regime candidates
   derived after their child Events/Gaps have been accepted.

These objects may overlap or relate without becoming a single winner-take-all
label.

## Burst and carrier States

- A selected Burst never deletes, splits, truncates, or triggers re-detection of
  independently supported Tonic, HFT, HF-irregular, or Broad-HFS geometry.
- Removing the Event track is therefore required to leave State geometry
  unchanged.
- The four-spike minimum and the frozen seed/local-background contrast rule
  apply to structure-first candidate generation, including HFS-local candidate
  mining. Immediate-edge contrast remains audit evidence rather than an extra
  universal hard gate. None of these candidate-generation checks is a post-hoc
  veto over a Burst established through another valid, versioned route.
- HFS-local contrast mining emits review-only `possible_burst` evidence. It does
  not promote itself automatically and does not demote ordinary canonical Burst
  evidence.

## Pause and Broad HFS

- Canonical Pause is excluded from Broad-HFS direct support and from direct-
  support performance denominators.
- The current v3.1 structural carrier cannot place canonical Pause inside one
  State envelope. Therefore the v3.2 overlay preserves higher context through
  explicit pause-interrupted episode links/relationships between independently
  supported Broad-HFS segments.
- A future provider-independent composer may represent a discontinuous episode
  envelope only under a new structural schema and separate scientific migration;
  it must continue to exclude Pause from direct support.
- Direct-support, envelope/relationship, fragmentation, and false-split metrics
  are reported separately.

## Recurrent Event regimes

- At least three accepted Burst Events may generate a recurrent-Bursting parent
  candidate.
- At least three accepted high-specificity long Pause entities, or at least five
  accepted ordinary Pause entities, may generate a recurrent-Pausing parent
  candidate.
- These rules run only after individual Event/Gap detection and do not discover,
  accept, reject, or retype a child.
- Every child is retained; recurrence uses a bounded one-pass envelope and cannot
  recursively consume its own parent output.
- Regimes are non-authoritative and cannot replace Tonic/Broad-HFS States or
  serve as biological truth before independent validation.

## Version and migration guard

- `stpd_multitrack_v3_1` remains readable under its original frozen validator.
- Scientific use of the revised compatibility policy must explicitly request or
  record `stpd_multitrack_v3_2_semantic_overlay`.
- Existing v3.1 signatures do not approve the v3.2 science overlay.
- Any future schema that changes State geometry, episode membership, or review
  transitions requires new contracts, fixtures, hashes, paired old/new results,
  and independent approval.
